Jorqeth: private commission settlement from a merchant's own records

Jorqeth privately determines creator commission eligibility from an agreed merchant record and releases the exact amount from merchant-funded escrow.

Claim boundary, above the fold. Jorqeth settles what the agreed merchant record shows, not universal attribution truth. The participating merchant stays the source of record. Synthetic records and testnet values only.
